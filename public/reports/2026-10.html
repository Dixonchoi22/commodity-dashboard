Trend Analysis Summary

The EU Food HICP index (Eurostat teicp010) stood at 100.91 in September 2026 (base 2025 = 100), with food inflation over the trailing twelve months subdued at +0.39% — well below the +1.48% seen in the July report. Beneath the calm headline, category swings are sharp: Tomato EU jumped +110.8% MoM, Potato processing EU +33.3% and Onion India +38.8%, while Sunflower Seed Russia and Prawn Norway both fell -31.0% MoM and Arabica Coffee ICE New York dropped -22.2%. Forward curves are carried over from the July Mintec export and will be refreshed when the new export arrives.

Executive Summary Highlights

Extreme price risk

Fresh produce leads the spikes: Tomato EU +110.8% MoM, Onion India +38.8% MoM and Potato processing EU +33.3% MoM (+300.0% YoY). Onion EU +100.0% and Onion India +116.5% YoY, Chilli India +75.8% and Turmeric India +59.4% YoY. Packaging resins stay elevated YoY — PP EU +46.1%, HDPE EU +37.9%, LDPE EU +34.1% — and grains are firm (Wheat CBOT +33.5%, Wheat Euronext +23.9% YoY).

Major opportunity

Deep YoY deflation opens contract-lock windows: Egg US -57.9%, Orange South Africa -57.3%, Hazelnuts Turkey -50.0%, Orange Juice Brazil -42.9% and Sunflower Seed Russia -41.2%. Dairy fats and cocoa are also lower (Butter EU -27.2%, Cocoa Bean ICE New York -22.3% YoY).

Oilseeds & coffee softening

Oilseed and coffee markets eased sharply on the month: Sunflower Seed Ukraine -27.1% MoM, Sunflower Oil Ukraine -13.6%, Palm Oil EU -10.1% and Arabica Coffee ICE New York -22.2%, while Apple US rose +13.1% MoM.
