Trend Analysis Summary

The EU Food HICP index (Eurostat teicp010) sat at 101.74 in May 2026 (base 2025 = 100), with YoY food inflation easing further to +1.48% — down from +1.99% in the April report and comfortably below the mid-2025 peak. The calm headline masks sharp category swings: Onion EU spiked +130.8% MoM (a supply-driven snap-back, still -49.2% YoY), Milk UK jumped +40.9% MoM, and cocoa turned higher again (Cocoa Bean ICE London +15.5% MoM). Forward curves reflect the Q3 2026 Mintec export (one-year outlook to mid-2027).

Executive Summary Highlights

Extreme price risk

Onion EU +130.8% MoM and Milk UK +40.9% MoM lead the monthly spikes, with cocoa rebounding (Cocoa Bean ICE London +15.5% MoM). Packaging resins remain sharply elevated YoY — PP EU +65.6%, HDPE EU +62.2%, LDPE EU +53.9% — alongside Chilli India +89.8% YoY.

Major opportunity

Deep YoY deflation opens contract-lock windows: Egg US -78.8%, Orange South Africa -52.8%, Butter EU -51.4% / Butter UK -48.4%, and Cocoa Bean ICE New York -51.4% YoY.

Grains & seafood softening

New-crop transition and weaker demand eased grains (CBOT Maize -10.3% MoM, CBOT Wheat -6.9% MoM) while seafood cooled (Haddock Norway -19.3% MoM, Salmon Norway -13.6% MoM). Heat and dryness across Western Europe remain a supply risk to watch.
